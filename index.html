FAQ

Questions about corporate gifting

Is corporate gifting with Sargam by Sanskriti budget-friendly?

Yes. Prices start at ₹24 per unit for pen boxes and ₹180 for boxed gift sets. All pricing is flexible, and discounts are applied based on order quantity, so larger orders cost less per piece.

Do you give discounts on bulk orders?

Yes. Discounts are applied based on order quantity. Share your item and quantity in the enquiry form and we will send a quote for your exact numbers.

Which cities do you deliver to?

We deliver across India, including Pune, Bengaluru (Bangalore), Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Noida, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Jaipur, Chandigarh and Indore.

Can you add our company logo to the gifts?

Yes. Every item can carry your logo through printing, engraving or embroidery, depending on the product. You approve a mockup before production.

What do you offer for new employee joining kits?

Our joining kits range from ₹1,470 to ₹3,750 per kit and include laptop bags or backpacks, bottles, diaries, pens and stands, packed in a branded box.

Do you make custom T-shirts and hoodies?

Yes. We offer polos, round-neck T-shirts, hoodies and oversized tees in many colours and fabrics. Apparel is priced by quantity and print.

Can you design custom T-shirts and merch for our company?

Yes. Sargam by Sanskriti has an in-house design team that takes your idea from concept to finished product: custom T-shirt and hoodie designs, event and fest merch, gift box packaging and joining kit branding. You approve the mockups before anything is produced.

How do I place an order?

Fill the enquiry form with the item, quantity and your contact, or call/WhatsApp +91 85113 56042. You can also download the catalogue and quote item codes.
